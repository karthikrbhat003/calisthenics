### 1. Dragon Hold (Dragon Flag Isometric Hold)
*Goal: Extreme anterior chain anti-extension and full-body lever rigidity supported on upper shoulders.*

  * *Execution: Grip bench/anchor behind head. Elevate torso, hips, and legs in a rigid straight diagonal line (~45 degrees) with glutes squeezed and toes pointed.*

  * *Set 3:* 1 Minute Hold *(Maximum mental focus through abdominal shaking)*

### 2. Supine Single-Leg Raise with 90-Degree Opposite Leg Hold
*Goal: Unilateral hip flexor isolation, anterior core anti-extension, and contralateral hamstring control.*

- [x] **[Supine Single-Leg Raise with 90-Degree Opposite Leg Hold](../../../exercise_library.md#supine-single-leg-raise-with-90-degree-opposite-leg-hold) — 30s Each Leg × 3 Sets**

  * *Set 1:* 30s Left Leg active / Right Leg 90° ➔ 30s Right Leg active / Left Leg 90°

### 3. Hollow Hold Flutter Kicks & Criss-Cross Flow
*Goal: Dynamic transverse abdominis and lower abdominal stamina under hollow body isometric tension.*

  * *Set 3:* 1 Minute Continuous *(Unbroken rhythm through full minute)*

### 4. Hanging Side-to-Side Knee Raises (Bar Windshield Knee Raises)
*Goal: Rotational core power, internal/external obliques, and grip endurance from a dead hang.*

- [x] **[Hanging Side-to-Side Knee Raises (Bar Windshield Knee Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-side-to-side-knee-raises-bar-windshield-knee-raises) — 1 Minute × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Hang from bar. Alternate pulling knees toward right elbow and left elbow in a controlled twisting arc for 60 seconds.*
  * *Set 1:* 1 Minute Continuous *(Zero kipping; strict oblique contraction)*
  * *Set 2:* 1 Minute Continuous *(High knee drive on both sides)*
  * *Set 3:* 1 Minute Continuous *(Strong forearm grip finish)*
