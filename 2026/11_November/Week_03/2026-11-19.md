## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Gymnastic Core Conditioning & Overhead Protocol

### 1. [Dragon Hold (Dragon Flag Isometric Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#dragon-hold-dragon-flag-isometric-hold)
*Form & Safety: Grip bench securely behind head, brace lats, squeeze glutes and quads together. Maintain a rigid straight diagonal line (~45°) without overarching the lower back for pain-free spinal integrity.*
- 3 Sets × 1 Minute Hold

### 2. [Supine Single-Leg Raise with 90-Degree Opposite Leg Hold](../../../exercise_library.md#supine-single-leg-raise-with-90-degree-opposite-leg-hold)
*Form & Safety: Keep lower back pressed flat into the floor (no arching). Lock one leg vertically at 90° while raising and lowering the opposite leg smoothly to a 1-inch hover.*
- 3 Sets × 30 Seconds Each Leg

### 3. [Hollow Hold Flutter Kicks & Criss-Cross Flow](../../../exercise_library.md#hollow-hold-flutter-kicks-criss-cross-flow)
*Form & Safety: Anchor lumbar spine firmly into the mat. Cycle 5 small vertical flutter kicks followed by 5 horizontal scissor pulses continuously with toes pointed.*
- 3 Sets × 1 Minute Continuous

### 4. [Hanging Side-to-Side Knee Raises (Bar Windshield Knee Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-side-to-side-knee-raises-bar-windshield-knee-raises)
*Form & Safety: Hang from bar with shoulders active. Alternate pulling knees toward right and left elbows in a controlled arc without swinging or kipping.*
- 3 Sets × 1 Minute Continuous

### 5. [Plank Single-Leg Raises (Plank Hip Extension Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#plank-single-leg-raises-plank-hip-extension-raises)
*Form & Safety: Maintain rigid forearm plank without letting hips sag or rotate. Lift one leg straight to hip level squeezing glute, then alternate sides.*
- 3 Sets × 30 Seconds Each Leg

### 6. [Core Compression (Pike/Straddle Compressions)](../../../exercise_library.md#core-compression-pikestraddle-compressions)
*Form & Safety: Sit in pike position with hands placed forward past knees. Actively compress core and lift straight legs off the ground without leaning torso backward.*
- 3 Sets × 1 Minute Continuous

### 7. [Overhead Weighted March Past (In-Place March with Straight Arms Overhead)](../../../exercise_library.md#overhead-weighted-march-past-in-place-march-with-straight-arms-overhead)
*Form & Safety: Press weight directly overhead with arms locked completely straight. Keep elbows extended, biceps by ears, and ribs pulled down. March in place driving knees up to 90 degrees with zero torso sway.*
- 3 Sets × 1 Minute Continuous
