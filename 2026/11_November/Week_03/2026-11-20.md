## ⚔️ Phase 2: Main Exercise — Team Game Day Calisthenics Relay
*Two-team calisthenics competitive showdown. Teams go head-to-head across alternating high-density relay rounds combining the five fundamental pillars of bodyweight strength.*

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   TWO-TEAM CALISTHENICS GAME DAY RELAY                 │
├────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PILLAR 1: VERTICAL PULL        │ PILLAR 2: HORIZONTAL PUSH             │

├────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤

├────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┤

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 🏆 Team Relay Matchups & Station Flow
- **Format:** Team Alpha vs. Team Bravo relay rounds. Each athlete executes their assigned station reps with unbroken form before tagging their teammate into the next movement.
- **Master Exercise Reference:** **[Team Game Day Calisthenics Relay (Pull-ups, Push-ups, Squats, Dips & Leg Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#team-game-day-calisthenics-relay-pull-ups-push-ups-squats-dips-leg-raises)**

#### Station Breakdown & Execution Standards:
1. **Vertical Pull:** **[Pull-ups (Standard)](../../../exercise_library.md#pull-ups-standard)**
   * *Standard:* Dead-hang start, hollow core, zero kip, chin clearing top of bar on every repetition.
2. **Horizontal Push:** **[Normal Push-up](../../../exercise_library.md#normal-push-up)**
   * *Standard:* Full chest-to-deck contact at bottom, rigid hollow plank line, full elbow lockout at top.
3. **Lower Body:** **[Standard Air Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#standard-air-squats)**
   * *Standard:* Hip crease breaking below parallel (below knee level), flat heels, tall torso throughout.
4. **Core Compression:** **[Hanging Knee Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-knee-raises)** / **[Hanging Leg Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-leg-raises)**
   * *Standard:* Controlled flexion from dead hang; knees or toes driven up without swinging momentum.
5. **Vertical Push:** **[Parallel Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-dips)**
   * *Standard:* Upper arms breaking 90 degrees at bottom; explosive lockout at top with shoulders depressed.
