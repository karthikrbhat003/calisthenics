## ⚔️ Phase 2: Main Exercise — Team Game Day Calisthenics Relay

### [Team Game Day Calisthenics Relay (Pull-ups, Push-ups, Squats, Dips & Leg Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#team-game-day-calisthenics-relay-pull-ups-push-ups-squats-dips-leg-raises)
*Form & Safety: Maintain strict competition standards without breaking form. Pain-free shoulder joint control, full lockout on pulls and dips, chest to floor on push-ups, and below-parallel depth on squats.*

- **Station Disciplines:**
  1. **[Pull-ups (Standard)](../../../exercise_library.md#pull-ups-standard)** — Dead hang to chin over bar; zero kipping.
  2. **[Parallel Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-dips)** — 90° elbow bend to full top lockout.
  3. **[Normal Push-up](../../../exercise_library.md#normal-push-up)** — Chest touches deck, rigid plank line.
  4. **[Hanging Knee Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-knee-raises)** / **[Hanging Leg Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-leg-raises)** — Controlled knee/toe elevation without body swing.
  5. **[Standard Air Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#standard-air-squats)** — Full depth below parallel with heels firmly planted.
- **Relay Volume:** 4 Total Competitive Relay Rounds (Team Alpha vs Team Bravo)
