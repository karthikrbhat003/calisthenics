*Goal: Frontal plane groin/adductor mobility, deep single-leg knee flexion, and ankle dorsiflexion.*

- [x] **[Cossack Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#cossack-squats) — 10 Reps × 3 Sets**

  * *Set 1:* 10 Reps (5 each side) *(Deep adductor opening)*
  * *Set 2:* 10 Reps (5 each side) *(Torso upright, heels firmly rooted)*
  * *Set 3:* 10 Reps (5 each side) *(Smooth transitions through center)*

### 4. Slow Pistol Squats (Tempo: 3s Down / 3s Up — 3 Sets)
*Goal: Extreme unilateral quad motor control, eliminating momentum to build bulletproof single-leg strength.*

- [x] **[Slow Pistol Squats (Tempo 3s Up / 3s Down)](../../../exercise_library.md#slow-pistol-squats-tempo-3s-up-3s-down) — 3 Sets Each Leg**
  * *Execution: Stand on one leg with opposite leg extended straight forward. Descend under strict control for 3 seconds, pause briefly at depth, then drive upward taking 3 full seconds to lockout.*
  * *Set 1:* Strict 3s down / 3s up *(Rock-solid balance and grounded heel)*
  * *Set 2:* Strict 3s down / 3s up *(Controlled ascent out of the hole without sticking points)*
  * *Set 3:* Strict 3s down / 3s up *(Tremendous single-leg quad burn on both sides)*
