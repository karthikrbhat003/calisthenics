## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Lower-Body Workout

### 1. [Descending Squat & Jump Squat Burnout Pyramid (120 Reps)](../../../exercise_library.md#jump-squats)
*Form & Safety: Land softly on ball of foot during jump squats, absorbing impact cleanly into deep squat. Full hip lockout and below-parallel depth on [Standard Air Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#standard-air-squats). Keep knees tracking in line with toes for pain-free knee joints.*

| Tier | [Jump Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#jump-squats) | [Standard Air Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#standard-air-squats) | Total Reps |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Round 1** | 30 Reps | 30 Reps | 60 Reps |
| **Round 2** | 20 Reps | 20 Reps | 40 Reps |
| **Round 3** | 10 Reps | 10 Reps | 20 Reps |

### 2. [Weighted Deadlifts](../../../exercise_library.md#weighted-deadlifts)
*Form & Safety: Keep spine neutral, lats engaged, and drive through heels to full glute lockout without hyperextending the lower back.*
- 3 Sets × 15 Reps

### 3. [Cossack Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#cossack-squats)
*Form & Safety: Wide stance. Sink hips back and down to working heel while straight leg rotates toes to ceiling. Keep working heel flat on the floor.*
- 3 Sets × 10 Reps (5 each side)

### 4. [Slow Pistol Squats (Tempo 3s Up / 3s Down)](../../../exercise_library.md#slow-pistol-squats-tempo-3s-up-3s-down)
*Form & Safety: Strict unilateral descent over 3 seconds, brief control at depth, and 3-second ascent. Keep chest upright and knee tracking over toes.*
- 3 Sets Each Leg (3s down / 3s up)

### 5. Restorative Hip & Calf Superset
*Form & Safety: Control pelvic shifts without twisting spine; maintain flat heels during deep squat hold.*
- **[Seated Butt Walks (Glute Walks / Pelvic Hip Walks)](../../../exercise_library.md#seated-butt-walks-glute-walks-pelvic-hip-walks):** 2 Sets × 1 Minute (30s forward / 30s backward)
- **[Deep Squat Calf Raises (Grounded Squat Heel Raises)](../../../exercise_library.md#deep-squat-calf-raises-grounded-squat-heel-raises):** 2 Sets × 1 Minute continuous heel raises
