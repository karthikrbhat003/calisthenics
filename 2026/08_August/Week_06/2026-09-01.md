### Exercises Checklist
- [x] **1. Bulgarian Split Squat**
  * *Target: 12 reps each leg x 3 sets. Rear foot elevated, drop back knee close to floor, upright posture.*
- [x] **2. Shrimp Squat**
  * *Target: 10 reps each leg x 3 sets. Hold back foot/ankle, lower back knee gently to touch floor, press up on one leg.*
- [x] **3. Superset: Box Step-Up + Single-Leg Deadlift (3 Rounds)**
  * *i. Box Step-Up:* 12 reps each leg. Drive through front foot onto elevated box without pushing off back toe.
  * *ii. Single-Leg Deadlift (SLDL):* 8 reps each leg. Hinge at hip, keep back straight, extend rear leg straight back.
- [x] **4. Single-Leg Glute Bridge**
  * *Target: 12 reps each side x 3 sets. Drive through heel, hold at top for 1s, keep hips level.*
- [x] **5. Single-Leg Calf Raises**
  * *Target: 25 reps on each side x 2 rounds. Full plantarflexion at top, deep stretch at bottom.*
