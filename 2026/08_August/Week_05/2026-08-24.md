### Holds Checklist
- [x] **i. Half Squat Hold**
  *Maintain weight in heels, torso upright, hips at a 45-degree angle.*
- [x] **ii. Chin-up at the Top Hold**
  *Pull chest to the bar under chin-up grip, squeeze shoulder blades together.*
- [x] **iii. Elbow Plank Hold**
  *Keep spine straight, core fully braced, and glutes engaged.*
- [x] **iv. Half Pull-up Position (Head to Bar)**
  *Hold position with arms bent at 90 degrees, keeping your head level with the bar.*
- [x] **v. Heel Elevated Half Squat Hold**
  *Squat down to 45 degrees while lifting your heels (balancing on balls of feet) to load the calves and quads.*
- [x] **vi. Active Hang with Knee Front (Core Engaged)**
  *Hang from the bar, depress shoulder blades (active hang), and hold knees up in front at 90 degrees.*
- [x] **vii. Superman Hold**
  *Lay prone on the floor, raise chest, arms, and legs off the ground, squeezing the posterior chain.*
