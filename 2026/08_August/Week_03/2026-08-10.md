### Exercises Checklist
- [x] **1. [Plank to Jump Between Hands (Full Down)](../../../exercise_library.md#plank-to-jump-between-hands-full-down)**
  *Focus on core stability during the plank and explosive power during the jump forward, lowering your hips fully.*
- [x] **2. [Pull-up Grip to Chin-up Grip](../../../exercise_library.md#pull-up-to-chin-up-grip-transition)**
  *Switching grip mid-set or between reps to target both the lats (pronated grip) and biceps/brachialis (supinated grip).*
- [x] **3. [Close Push-up to Wide Push-up](../../../exercise_library.md#close-push-up)**
  *Alternating hand placement to shift emphasis between triceps/inner chest (close) and outer pectorals (wide).*
- [x] **4. [Squats with Thrust While Coming Up](../../../exercise_library.md#squats-with-thrust)**
  *Perform a squat holding weights in hands at shoulder level, and press them straight up overhead while rising out of the squat.*
- [x] **5. [In-Place Running (Jump Rope Simulation)](../../../exercise_library.md#in-place-running-jump-rope-style)**
  *Mimic jumping rope while running/bouncing in place to maintain high frequency and timing.*
- [x] **6. [Tuck Jump Burpees](../../../exercise_library.md#tuck-jump-burpees)**
  *Full burpee with an explosive tuck jump (drawing knees to chest) at the top.*
