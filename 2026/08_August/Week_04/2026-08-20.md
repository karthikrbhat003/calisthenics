## 🧘 Phase 3: Post-Workout Mobility Routine
*Goal: Mobilize the spine, open up internal hip rotators, and improve ankle ankle dorsiflexion.*

- [x] **1. [Wall Arm Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#wall-arm-raises)**
  * **Target:** 10 rounds.
  * **Execution:** Keep spine flat against the wall, raise arms from T-position overhead and back while maintaining wall contact.
- [x] **2. [Thoracic Spine Stretch](../../../exercise_library.md#thoracic-wall-stretch)**
  * **Target:** 10 rounds.
  * **Execution:** Kneeling on the ground, stretching chest/spine down against the wall.
- [x] **3. [Hip Thrust with 90-90 Stretch](../../../exercise_library.md#hip-thrust-with-90-90-stretch)**
  * **Target:** 10 reps each side.
  * **Execution:** Transition from a 90-90 hip stretch to driving hips up into a thrust.
- [x] **4. [Hip Internal Rotation](../../../exercise_library.md#hip-internal-wall-rotation)**
  * **Target:** 10 reps each side.
  * **Execution:** Place knee against wall in an elevated position, rotate thigh internally while keeping upper torso stable.
- [x] **5. [Ankle Mobility](../../../exercise_library.md#ankle-mobility-lunge-drive)**
  * **Execution:** From a lunge position, drive the knee as far forward over the toes as possible without letting the heel lift.
