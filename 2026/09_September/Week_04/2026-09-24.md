## ⏱️ Phase 2: Tempo Time-Under-Tension (TUT) Strength
*Goal: Maximum motor unit recruitment and eccentric tendon conditioning using strict timed tempos.*

- [x] **1. [Slow Chin-ups (7s Up / 7s Down Tempo)](../../../exercise_library.md#slow-chin-ups-7s-up-7s-down)**
  * *Target: 2 reps per set x 3 sets (6 total reps).*
  * *[Form: Underhand grip. Pull smoothly taking a full 7 seconds to reach chin over bar, pause at the top, and take a full 7 seconds to descend back to a dead hang.](../../../exercise_library.md#dead-hang)*
- [x] **2. [Slow Push-ups (15s Up / 15s Down Tempo)](../../../exercise_library.md#slow-push-ups-15s-up-15s-down)**
  * *Target: 2 reps per set x 3 sets (6 total reps).*
  * *Form: Strict hollow-body plank. Lower chest to floor over a full 15 seconds, hover 1 inch off the ground, then press up over a full 15 seconds to locked protraction.*
