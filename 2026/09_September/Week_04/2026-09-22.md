### Circuit Exercises Checklist
- [x] **1. [Inchworm Push-up](../../../exercise_library.md#inchworm-push-up)**
  * *Target: 6 reps per round x 3 rounds (18 total reps).*
  * *Form: Hinge at hips, walk hands forward into a tall plank, execute a full depth chest-to-floor push-up, and walk hands back with straight legs.*
- [x] **2. [Donkey Kick to Plank](../../../exercise_library.md#donkey-kick-to-plank-return)**
  * *Target: 10 reps per round x 3 rounds (30 total reps).*
  * *Form: From high plank, hop or kick feet up dynamically with hips lifting over shoulders, landing softly back into a braced plank position.*
- [x] **3. [Plank Dumbbell Rows (Alternating Renegade Rows)](../../../exercise_library.md#plank-dumbbell-rows-renegade-rows)**
  * *Target: 10 reps each side (20 total reps per round) x 3 rounds (60 total reps).*
  * *Form: Solid push-up position gripping dumbbells. Row one dumbbell to hip pocket without rotating hips or torso, lower under control, and switch sides.*
- [x] **4. [Supine Dumbbell Press](../../../exercise_library.md#supine-dumbbell-floor-mat-press)**
  * *Target: 12 reps per round x 3 rounds (36 total reps).*
  * *Form: Lie flat on back (supine) with knees bent, press dumbbells smoothly up over mid-chest, squeezing pecs and controlling the descent.*
