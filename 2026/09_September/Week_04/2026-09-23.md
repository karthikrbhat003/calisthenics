### Exercises Checklist
- [x] **1. [Box Jumps or Over-the-Box Jumps](../../../exercise_library.md#over-the-box-jumps-clearance-box-jumps)**
  * *Target: 10 reps x 3 sets (30 total reps).*
  * *Form: Powerful counter-movement swing, explode up onto the plyo box (or clear cleanly over the box), landing softly in a controlled quarter-to-half squat.*
- [x] **2. [Kneeling Jump to Squat Jump and Up](../../../exercise_library.md#kneeling-jump-to-squat-jump-explosive-hip-snap-pop)**
  * *Target: 15 reps x 3 sets (45 total reps).*
  * *Form: Start kneeling tall on a padded surface. Swing arms back and powerfully thrust hips forward to snap feet under into a low squat, then immediately explode vertically into a full-height squat jump before resetting to kneeling.*
- [x] **3. [Skater's Squats (Airborne Single-Leg Squats)](../../../exercise_library.md#skaters-squats-airborne-single-leg-squat)**
  * *Target: 8 reps each side x 3 sets (24 reps per leg).*
  * *Form: Stand on one leg with the other knee bent behind at 90 degrees. Lower down into a single-leg squat until the back knee lightly taps the floor behind your heel (without resting weight), then drive through the front foot to stand tall.*
- [x] **4. Plyometric Conditioning Superset (3 Rounds)**
  * *Target: 3 continuous rounds:*
    * **i) [Running in Place (High Knees)](../../../exercise_library.md#high-knees):** 30 seconds (High-cadence foot turnover / high knees)
    * **ii) [Lateral Jump Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#lateral-jump-squats-bound-squat-jumps):** 30 seconds (Explosive side-to-side bound into a deep squat absorption)
- [x] **5. [Single-Leg Hops (Unilateral Elasticity Bounces)](../../../exercise_library.md#single-leg-forward-hops-one-leg-front-jump)**
  * *Target: 30 seconds each leg x 3 rounds.*
  * *Form: Stay on the ball of one foot, bouncing continuously with short ground contact times while maintaining upright posture and knee tracking.*
