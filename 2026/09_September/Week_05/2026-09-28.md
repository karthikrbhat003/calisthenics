### Exercises Checklist
- [x] **1. [Mike Tyson Push-ups (Quad-Spring Push-ups)](../../../exercise_library.md#mike-tyson-push-ups-quad-spring-push-ups)**
  * *Target: 10 reps x 3 sets (30 total reps).*
  * *Form: Start in high plank. Bend knees and push hips all the way back toward heels with knees hovering 1-2 inches above ground (loaded beast). Explode forward directly into a deep chest-to-floor push-up and press back to spring position in one fluid, rhythmic motion.*
- [x] **2. [Unbroken Dip & Leg Raise Complex](../../../exercise_library.md#unbroken-dip-hangingsupport-leg-raise-complex)**
  * *Target: 3 unbroken rounds (30 dips + 30 leg raises total).*
  * *Form: Mount parallel bars: perform 5 full-range dips, immediately transition to 10 hanging/support leg raises without dismounting, and finish with another 5 crisp dips.*
- [x] **3. [Isometric Halfway Push-up Complex (90-Degree Hover Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#isometric-push-up-halfway-hold-90-degree-hover-hold)**
  * *Target: 3 rounds (30 push-ups + 30s isometric hold total).*
  * *Form: Perform 5 strict push-ups, lower to a 90-degree elbow halfway hover position and hold for a strict 10 seconds, then immediately complete 5 more push-ups to lockout.*
- [x] **4. Timed Core & Shoulder Conditioning Superset (4 Sets)**
  * *Target: 4 continuous rounds:*
    * **i) [Plank In-and-Out Leg Jumps (Plank Jacks)](../../../exercise_library.md#plank-in-and-out-leg-jumps-plank-jacks):** 1 minute (Fast, rhythmic core-braced bounding jumping feet wide and together).
    * **ii) [Dumbbell Supine-to-Overhead Full-Arc Lateral Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#full-arc-overhead-dumbbell-lateral-raises-180-straight-arm-arc):** 1 minute (Holding dumbbells with palms facing forward/upward, perform straight-arm 180° lateral raises extending all the way overhead until dumbbells touch, then lower slowly with locked elbows).
