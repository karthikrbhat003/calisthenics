## 🧘 Phase 3: Loaded Mobility & Posterior Chain Restoration
*Goal: Decompress the shoulder capsule, open deep hip rotators, and mobilize the hamstrings and sciatic nerve.*

- [x] **1. [Prone Swimmer Hovers (Shoulder CARs & Extension Arc)](../../../exercise_library.md#prone-swimmer-hovers-shoulder-cars-extension-arc)**
  * *Execution: Forehead on the floor. Lift both arms overhead off the floor, sweep them wide in a continuous hover arc around to the hips, and cross/join hands behind lower back without touching the ground. Reverse smoothly.*
  * *Target: 8–10 fluid repetitions.*
- [x] **2. [90-90 Good Mornings (90-90 Hip Hinge)](../../../exercise_library.md#90-90-good-mornings-90-90-hip-hinge)**
  * *Execution: Seated in 90-90 hip orientation. Hinge torso forward with a flat neutral spine over the front lead knee.*
  * *Target: 10 reps per side.*
- [x] **3. [Elephant Walks (Standing Alternating Hamstring Floss)](../../../exercise_library.md#elephant-walks-standing-alternating-hamstring-floss)**
  * *Execution: Standing forward fold with hands on ground/blocks. Alternately bend one knee while driving the opposite heel down to lock the knee and floss the hamstring.*
  * *Target: 20–30 total pedals in place.*
- [x] **4. [Forward Fold Torso Twist (Standing Single-Hand Ground Reach & Spine Rotation)](../../../exercise_library.md#forward-fold-torso-twist-standing-single-hand-ground-reach-spine-rotation)**
  * *Execution: From the elephant walk forward fold position, keep one hand grounded, extend the opposite leg straight, and rotate torso toward the ceiling reaching the free arm high.*
  * *Target: 5 deep-breath holds per side.*
- [x] **5. [Extended Forward Reach Hamstring Stretch (Pike Downward Reach)](../../../exercise_library.md#extended-forward-reach-hamstring-stretch-pike-downward-reach)**
  * *Execution: Deep forward fold with locked knees. Walk both hands far forward on the floor while shifting hips back to create maximum traction along the entire posterior chain.*
  * *Target: 45–60 seconds deep diaphragmatic hold.*
