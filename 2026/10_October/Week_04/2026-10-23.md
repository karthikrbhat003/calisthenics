## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Inversion Skill, Obstacle Agility & Strength Circuit

### 1. [Headstand Flow Practice (Tripod & Forearm Inversion Flow)](../../../exercise_library.md#headstand-flow-practice-tripod-forearm-inversion-flow)

- ~15 Minutes Dedicated Practice

### 2. [Floor Is Lava (Calisthenics Obstacle & Rail Balance Traversal)](../../../exercise_library.md#floor-is-lava-calisthenics-obstacle-rail-balance-traversal)
*Form & Safety: Maintain active balance on rails, bars, and boxes without touching the ground. Emphasize soft landings and dynamic foot placement.*
- ~10–12 Minutes Continuous Movement

### 3. 4-Round Full-Body Conditioning Circuit
*Form & Safety: Maintain clean lockout and controlled tempos across all stations with minimal rest between movements.*

| Station | Exercise | Volume (4 Rounds) | Execution Focus |
|:---:|---|:---:|---|
| **i** | **[Chin-ups (Standard)](../../../exercise_library.md#chin-ups-standard)** | 5 Reps | Full dead hang to chin clearing bar; strict biceps and lat drive. |
| **ii** | **[Parallel Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-dips)** | 5 Reps | 90° elbow descent, explosive triceps lockout. |
| **iii** | **[Hanging Knee Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#hanging-knee-raises)** | 10 Reps | Knees driven tightly to chest; zero swinging. |
| **iv** | **[Reverse Burpees (Candlestick Roll-Back to Jump Squat)](../../../exercise_library.md#reverse-burpees-candlestick-roll-back-to-jump-squat)** | 10 Reps | Roll smoothly onto upper back into candlestick, roll forward into vertical jump. |

- **Circuit Total:** 4 Complete Rounds (20 Chin-ups, 20 Dips, 40 Knee Raises, 40 Reverse Burpees)
