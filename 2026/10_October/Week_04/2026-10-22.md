## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Lower-Body & Core Workout

### 1. Isometric Core & Quadriceps Superset (4 Rounds)
*Form & Safety: Hollow body plank line without sagging; back flat against wall with thighs strictly parallel (90°) for pain-free knee and spinal loading.*
- **[Elbow Plank](../../../exercise_library.md#elbow-plank):** 4 Sets × 30 Seconds Hold
- **[Wall Sit (Isometric Wall Squat Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#wall-sit-isometric-wall-squat-hold):** 4 Sets × 30 Seconds Hold

### 2. Anti-Rotation & Split Squat Isometric Superset (4 Rounds)
*Form & Safety: Keep hips square to floor during single-arm plank; hover rear knee 1 inch off floor with upright chest on lunges.*
- **[Single Hand Planks](../../../exercise_library.md#single-hand-planks):** 4 Sets × 15 Seconds Each Arm
- **[Isometric Hover Lunge Hold (Floating Knee Lunge)](../../../exercise_library.md#isometric-hover-lunge-hold-floating-knee-lunge):** 4 Sets × 15 Seconds Each Leg

### 3. [Pistol Squat Bottom Position Hold (Single-Leg Isometric Bottom Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#pistol-squat-bottom-position-hold-single-leg-isometric-bottom-hold)
*Form & Safety: Single-leg bottom squat with non-working leg extended straight; maintain balance on flat grounded foot without knee collapse.*
- 2 Sets × 20 Seconds Each Leg

### 4. Plyometric In-and-Out Squats & Pulses (3 Rounds)
*Form & Safety: Land softly on balls of feet, absorbing shock cleanly into deep squat. Maintain continuous pulses in middle range without lockout.*
- **[Narrow to Wide Jump Squats (In-and-Out Squat Jumps)](../../../exercise_library.md#narrow-to-wide-jump-squats-in-and-out-squat-jumps):** 3 Sets × 20 Reps
- **[Half Squat Pulses](../../../exercise_library.md#half-squat-pulses):** 3 Sets × 20 Pulses

### 5. Prisoner Squats & Glute Bridge Superset (2 Rounds)
*Form & Safety: Hands behind head, chest tall, full below-parallel depth. Drive through heels on hip thrusts squeezing glutes at top.*
- **[Prisoner Squats](../../../exercise_library.md#prisoner-squats):** 2 Sets × 15 Reps
- **[Glute Bridge Thrusts (Floor Hip Thrusts)](../../../exercise_library.md#glute-bridge-thrusts-floor-hip-thrusts):** 2 Sets × 15 Reps

### 6. Loaded Gait & Overhead Unilateral March
*Form & Safety: Stay low in athletic stance during lateral walks. Keep dumbbell pressed vertically overhead with locked elbow during high-knee marching.*
- **[Lateral Walks](../../../exercise_library.md#lateral-walks):** 40 Seconds Continuous
- **[Single-Arm Overhead Dumbbell March (Waiter's March)](../../../exercise_library.md#single-arm-overhead-dumbbell-march-waiters-march):** 30 Seconds Each Arm

### 7. [Standing Bilateral Calf Raises](../../../exercise_library.md#standing-bilateral-calf-raises)
*Form & Safety: Press straight up through big toes into full ankle extension; control the eccentric lowering.*
- 2 Sets × 20 Reps
