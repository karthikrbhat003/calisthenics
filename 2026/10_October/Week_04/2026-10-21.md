## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Calisthenics Strength & Skill Workout

### 1. [Muscle-up](../../../exercise_library.md#muscle-up) *(Alternatives: [High Pull-ups](../../../exercise_library.md#high-pull-ups-explosive-bar-high-pulls) / [Chin-up to Muscle-up Grip Switch](../../../exercise_library.md#chin-up-to-muscle-up-grip-switch))*
*Form & Safety: Hollow body arch-to-hollow snap, explosive vertical pull, and smooth turnover over the bar into clean straight-bar lockout without shoulder impingement.*
- 3 Sets × 3 Muscle-ups *(or 3 Sets × 6 Explosive High Pulls / Grip Switches)*

### 2. [Parallel Bar Support Walks (Dip Bar Walks)](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-support-walks-dip-bar-walks)
*Form & Safety: Mount parallel bars in locked-arm support with shoulders fully depressed. Walk hand-over-hand forward and backward with zero torso swinging.*
- 3 Sets × 2 Full Traversals (Forward & Backward)

### 3. [Straight Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#straight-bar-dips)
*Form & Safety: Overhand grip on single bar. Lean torso forward with hollow legs, descend until chest lightly touches bar (90° elbow flexion), and press out to lockout.*
- 3 Sets × 8 Reps

### 4. Upper-Body Antagonist Volume Superset (Chin-ups + Dips)
*Form & Safety: Full dead hang to chin over bar on chin-ups; deep 90° descent and crisp lockout on dips. Keep movements strict and controlled.*
- **[Chin-ups (Standard)](../../../exercise_library.md#chin-ups-standard):** 2 Sets × 10 Reps
- **[Parallel Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-dips):** 2 Sets × 10 Reps

### 5. Posterior Chain & Scapular Extension Finisher
*Form & Safety: Squeeze glutes and lower back on superman holds; actively retract shoulder blades and drive hands toward ceiling on prone arm reaches.*
- **[Superman Hold](../../../exercise_library.md#superman-hold):** 2 Sets × 15 Reps / 15s Hold
- **[Prone Arm Raise & Reach Back with Lift](../../../exercise_library.md#prone-arm-raise-reach-back-with-lift):** 2 Sets × 15 Reps
