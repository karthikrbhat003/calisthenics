### 2. Parallel Bar Support Walks (Dip Bar Walks)
*Goal: Scapular depression endurance, straight-arm stability, and rotational anti-wobble control under bodyweight.*

- [x] **[Parallel Bar Support Walks (Dip Bar Walks)](../../../exercise_library.md#parallel-bar-support-walks-dip-bar-walks) — 2 Rounds × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Mount parallel bars in a locked-arm support. Walk forward hand-over-hand to end of bars, then walk backward to start (1 round). Perform 2 full traversals per set.*
  * *Set 1:* 2 traversals *(Strict locked elbows, depressed shoulders)*
  * *Set 2:* 2 traversals *(Steady tempo, zero torso swaying)*
  * *Set 3:* 2 traversals *(Maximum scapular push into bars)*

### 3. Straight Bar Dips
*Goal: Master the forward lean, shoulder extension, and triceps press-out required at the top of a muscle-up.*

- [x] **[Straight Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#straight-bar-dips) — 8 Reps × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Grip a single straight bar with an overhand grip. Lean torso forward with hollow-body legs, lower until lower chest lightly grazes bar, and press to full lockout.*
  * *Set 1:* 8 Reps *(Controlled eccentric descent)*
  * *Set 2:* 8 Reps *(Consistent 90° elbow flexion depth)*
  * *Set 3:* 8 Reps *(Strong terminal elbow extension)*
