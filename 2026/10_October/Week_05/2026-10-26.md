### 3. Straight Bar Dips
*Goal: Reinforce front support transition strength, chest stretch, and triceps press-out on a single bar.*

- [x] **[Straight Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#straight-bar-dips) — 10 Reps × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Overhand grip on a single bar. Lean torso forward with hollow legs, lower until lower chest lightly touches bar, and press out to full elbow extension.*
  * *Set 1:* 10 Reps *(Controlled 2s eccentric descent)*
  * *Set 2:* 10 Reps *(Consistent depth touching chest to bar)*
  * *Set 3:* 10 Reps *(Strong terminal triceps lockout)*

---

### 4. Bodyweight Tricep Extensions (Bar Skull Crushers)
*Goal: Direct hypertrophy and tendon loading for the triceps brachii long head under bodyweight angles.*

- [x] **[Bodyweight Tricep Extensions](../../../exercise_library.md#bodyweight-tricep-extensions) — 10 Reps × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Hands on waist-height bar, body angled in a rigid plank. Bend only at the elbows to lower forehead beneath the bar, then drive through palms to lockout.*
  * *Set 1:* 10 Reps *(Strict elbow tracking; zero shoulder hinge)*
  * *Set 2:* 10 Reps *(Deep stretch at bottom position)*
  * *Set 3:* 10 Reps *(Peak triceps contraction)*

---

### 5. Banded Low-Anchor Isometric Pull Hold
*Goal: Time-under-tension isometric loading for latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, and lower trapezius in full retraction.*

- [x] **[Banded Low-Anchor Isometric Pull Hold (Resistance Band Row Isometric Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#banded-low-anchor-isometric-pull-hold-resistance-band-row-isometric-hold) — 30s Hold × 3 Sets**
  * *Execution: Heavy resistance band anchored low. Pull elbows tight past ribs into maximal retraction and hold statically for 30 seconds.*
  * *Set 1:* 30s Hold *(Depressed shoulders, tall chest)*
  * *Set 2:* 30s Hold *(Sustained rhomboid and lat squeeze)*
  * *Set 3:* 30s Hold *(Maximal effort isometric contraction)*
