## 🔥 Phase 2: Main Calisthenics Strength & Skill Workout

### 1. [Skin the Cat](../../../exercise_library.md#skin-the-cat) *(Alternative: [Pull-over (Bar)](../../../exercise_library.md#pull-over-bar))*
*Form & Safety: Rotate through arms into full German hang under control. Maintain active hollow body and avoid abrupt drops to ensure pain-free shoulder extension.*
- 3 Sets × 5 Reps

### 2. [Muscle-up](../../../exercise_library.md#muscle-up) *(Alternatives: [High Pull-ups](../../../exercise_library.md#high-pull-ups-explosive-bar-high-pulls) / [Standard Pull-ups](../../../exercise_library.md#pull-ups-standard))*
*Form & Safety: Explosive vertical pull with fast turnover over the bar into clean straight-arm lockout. If substituting, perform full-range high pulls or strict dead-hang pull-ups.*
- 3 Sets × 3 Muscle-ups *(or 3 Sets × 8 High Pulls / Strict Pull-ups)*

### 3. [Straight Bar Dips](../../../exercise_library.md#straight-bar-dips)
*Form & Safety: Overhand grip on a single bar. Lean torso forward slightly with hollow legs, lower until lower chest touches the bar, and press to full elbow extension.*
- 3 Sets × 10 Reps

### 4. [Bodyweight Tricep Extensions (Bar Skull Crushers)](../../../exercise_library.md#bodyweight-tricep-extensions)
*Form & Safety: Hands on waist-height bar, body in a rigid plank. Hinge only at the elbows to lower forehead beneath the bar, driving through palms to lockout.*
- 3 Sets × 10 Reps

### 5. [Banded Low-Anchor Isometric Pull Hold](../../../exercise_library.md#banded-low-anchor-isometric-pull-hold-resistance-band-row-isometric-hold)
*Form & Safety: Heavy resistance band anchored low. Pull elbows past ribs into maximal scapular retraction and hold statically with depressed shoulders.*
- 3 Sets × 30 Seconds Hold
