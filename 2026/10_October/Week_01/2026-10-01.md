## 🧘 Phase 3: Loaded Mobility & Joint Restoration
*Goal: Strengthen posterior scapular rotators (I-Y-T), decompress thoracic spine, expand hip rotational range, and dynamically floss hamstrings.*

- [x] **1. [Prone I - Y - T Raises (with 10s Isometric Final Rep Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#prone-i-y-t-raises-with-isometric-holds)**
  * *Target: 10 reps of each position + 10s hold on rep 10.*
  * *Form: Lie prone (face down). Perform 10 reps in "I" position (arms along sides/overhead), 10 in "Y" position (45° overhead thumbs up for lower traps), and 10 in "T" position (90° for mid traps/rhomboids). Hold the 10th rep of each letter at peak contraction for a full 10 seconds.*
- [x] **2. [Prone Scorpion Stretch (Shoulders Flat on Floor)](../../../exercise_library.md#prone-scorpion-stretch-flat-shoulders)**
  * *Target: 15 reps alternating sides.*
  * *Form: Lie face down with arms outstretched in a "T" and both shoulders glued to the ground. Lift one leg, bend the knee, and reach the foot across your body toward the opposite hand, opening the hip flexor and lumbar/thoracic spine while keeping shoulders grounded.*
- [x] **3. [Tabletop Single-Leg Extended Hip Internal & External Rotation](../../../exercise_library.md#tabletop-single-leg-extended-hip-internal-external-rotation)**
  * *Target: 10 reps each leg + 10s isometric hold on rep 10.*
  * *Form: From quadruped tabletop, extend one leg straight back parallel to the floor. Without shifting pelvis, rotate the entire leg internally (toes pointing inward) and externally (toes pointing outward) from the hip joint. Hold the 10th rep in full rotation for 10 seconds.*
- [x] **4. [Medicine Ball Weighted Thoracic Spine Mobility (with 10s Final Hold)](../../../exercise_library.md#medicine-ball-weighted-thoracic-spine-extension-mobility)**
  * *Target: 15 reps + 10s hold on rep 15.*
  * *Form: Place a medicine ball beneath the thoracic spine (mid-back) while lying supine. Hold a light weight with both hands, reach arms back overhead into thoracic extension over the ball, and smoothly return. Hold the 15th rep in deep extension for 10 seconds.*
- [x] **5. [Supine Band-Assisted Hamstring Dynamic Floss & Stretch](../../../exercise_library.md#supine-band-assisted-hamstring-dynamic-floss-stretch)**
  * *Target: 10 reps each leg.*
  * *Form: Lie flat on back with one leg straight on the ground. Wrap a resistance band around the foot of the other leg. Pull the knee tight to the chest with knee bent, then actively extend and straighten the leg against the band toward the ceiling to floss the sciatic nerve and stretch the hamstring. Hold briefly and repeat.*
